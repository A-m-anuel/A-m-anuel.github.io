Why I built this website as a public CV

A simple site can explain your work faster than a resume alone.

I wanted a single link I could share on LinkedIn, GitHub, and job applications without making people dig through multiple platforms. A personal site gives context quickly: who I am, what kind of work I care about, and where my strongest work lives.

For me, that means bringing together three things in one place: my CV, a few selected cybersecurity projects, and the writing that shows how I learn and communicate technical ideas. The goal is not to say everything. The goal is to make the important things easy to find.

I also wanted the design to feel modern and easy to scan on both desktop and mobile. Recruiters move fast, so the structure matters: clear sections, strong calls to action, and direct links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Medium, and the full resume.
